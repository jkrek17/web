  • Gridded Cyclone Phase Space in AWIPS/article/

    A short academic article on computing Hart's cyclone phase space as a gridded AWIPS II D2D derived parameter, with reproducible synthetic-field figures.

  • Cyclone Phase Space, live/cyclone/

    A live global map of the gridded cyclone phase space products: the Hart class of every closed low, MSLP, and the thermal asymmetry and lower and upper thermal wind fields, with every tracked low and its phase diagrams, animated over the latest model cycle.

  • Cyclone Phase Space for the Forecast Desk/forecasters/

    A plain-language guide to the gridded cyclone phase space products in AWIPS D2D: what the three numbers mean, how to read the class map, a worked real case, and where the products mislead.
