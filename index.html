Projects

10 projects, each listed by its own page title and description.

  • Hurricane Force Extratropical Low Archive/hf-lows/

    Archive and climatology of hurricane force extratropical lows in the North Atlantic and North Pacific.

  • High Seas Draft/high-seas/

    A first-guess OPC High Seas Forecast (Pacific and Atlantic) written automatically from the marine wind page's grids and GFS-Wave seas each model run, with its warning areas on a globe. For forecasters to edit, not an official product.

  • NWS Local Storm Reports - Interactive Map test: main/lsr/

    Interactive map of National Weather Service Local Storm Reports. View real-time and historical storm reports across the United States.

    • NWS Local Storm Reports - Interactive Map test: next/lsr/next/

      Interactive map of National Weather Service Local Storm Reports. View real-time and historical storm reports across the United States.

  • Marine Wind · Global model blend/marine-wind/

    A global blended 10 m wind forecast from GFS, ECMWF and AIFS plus about 130 ensemble members, with where the models disagree on gale, storm and hurricane force, the ensemble's chance of each, and lone-model storm signals.

    • Marine Wind · Forecast recipe/marine-wind/recipe/

      The forecast recipe the marine wind project has arrived at: how to turn GFS, ECMWF, the AI models and their ensembles into the most likely wind, warning areas and lows, each step with its held-out score against ASCAT.

  • Oceanic Forecast · NDFD & OPC Surface/ndfd/

    Oceanic weather forecast: NDFD wave and wind grids with OPC surface fronts and isobars on a 3D globe.

  • Phillies 2026 Lineup Study/phillies/
    • Phillies 2027 Offseason Plan/phillies/offseason/
  • Research Scores/research-scores/

    Weekly scores of the marine forecast desk's research studies (day 5-7 pressure, warning forecast): the latest 4 weeks against each study's original interval, with a drift flag and a trend line for every score. North Atlantic and North Pacific.

  • Marine Wind Verification/verification/

    How GFS, ECMWF, AIFS, their ensembles and the marine wind page's blends have verified against ASCAT, buoys, ships and the GDAS and ECMWF analyses, North Atlantic and North Pacific, +24 to +120 h.

  • Greensboro Wedding Weather/wedding-weather/

    Rain chances and temperatures for Greensboro, NC on Friday evening 23 and Saturday afternoon 24 October 2026, updated each morning.

  • Wind · GFS particle forecast/wind/

Experimental guidance, not an official forecast. For warnings see the Ocean Prediction Center.
