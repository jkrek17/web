Public Information Statements (PNS)

What are PNS? Public Information Statements are detailed text products issued by NWS Weather Forecast Offices containing weather observations, storm summaries, and significant weather data.

Data includes: Snow totals, rainfall amounts, wind gusts, temperatures, ice accumulation, flood reports, and other significant weather measurements from observers and automated stations.

PNS reports with embedded coordinates are plotted at their exact location. Others are placed at the issuing WFO office. Click markers to view full details.

 Welcome to NWS Local Storm Reports

                    
                

                

                    

                        
🗺️

                        
Interactive Storm Report Map

                        
View real-time and historical storm reports from the National Weather Service across the United States, Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico.

                        
Tip: Click any marker to see detailed report information.

                    

                    

                        
📅

                        
Quick Date Presets

                        
Use the quick presets (Last 6h through Last Week) for instant loading, or choose Custom for full control. The −1 Day / +1 Day buttons shift by one calendar day: with a preset they snap to 12:00–12:00 UTC; in Custom they keep your start and end times.

                        
Tip: Date presets automatically fetch data - no need to click "Get Data"!

                    

                    

                        
📍

                        
Filter by Location

                        
Filter reports by state, region, or view all US data. The map will automatically zoom to your selection.

                        
Tip: Click summary items in "Current View" to jump to filter sections.

                    

                    

                        
🌩️

                        
Weather Type Filters

                        
Filter by specific weather types (Rain, Snow, Hail, Tornado, etc.) or use quick filters like "Severe", "Winter", "Precip", or "Tropical".

                        
Tip: Click "All" or "None" buttons to quickly toggle all weather types.

                    

                    

                        
📋

                        
Public Information Statements (PNS)

                        
Enable PNS to see detailed weather observations from NWS offices. PNS reports include snow totals, rainfall amounts, wind gusts, temperatures, ice accumulation, and other significant measurements.

                        
Tip: PNS reports with coordinates appear at exact locations. Click any marker to view the full statement text with all observer details.

                    

                    

                        
⚡

                        
Playback, Live & Sharing

                        
"Playback" replays the current date range over time with radar and the warnings in effect. "Live" follows the latest reports, radar and warnings, refreshing every minute. Use "Share" to copy a link with your current filters, or "Export" to download data.

                        
Tip: Keyboard shortcuts: G (Get Data), C (Clear), S (Share), E (Export), ? (Help)
