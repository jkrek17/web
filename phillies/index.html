Beyond the order

Six changes that fit this roster

The order is worth a handful of runs. These target the larger levers: pitch decisions and batted-ball shape. For the personnel side (who to sign and trade for 2027), see the 2027 offseason plan.

Hunt one early-count lane

Trea Turner, Kyle Schwarber, Justin Crawford gained the most OPS when the plate appearance ended on the first pitch. Give them one pitch type and location to attack before two strikes.

Reduce expansion

Edmundo Sosa, Justin Crawford, Brandon Marsh carried the highest chase rates among Phillies with 200+ PA. Narrow their zones, especially with runners on.

Turn impact into lift

Justin Crawford, Alec Bohm, Adolis García paired hard contact with the most ground balls. Small launch-angle gains convert exit velocity into extra bases.

Break up whiff clusters

Adolis García, Kyle Schwarber, Bryce Harper missed most often per swing. Keep a contact bat between them in the order.

Price the platoon bench

Against lefties the best-hitting legal nine starts Luis Arraez, Adolis García over Brandon Marsh, Bryson Stott, worth +16.3 runs per 162 before defense. That is the size of glove it takes to justify the regulars.

Grade the process weekly

Track early-zone attack rate, chase with runners in scoring position and hard airborne contact before reacting to short-term OPS.

Transparency

Methods and limits

Data. Baseball Savant Statcast via pybaseball, regular season 2026-03-01 through 2026-09-30: 183,849 batter-completed MLB plate appearances, 6,079 by Phillies hitters. Runner events such as caught stealing are not plate appearances here.

Run values. A 2026 run-expectancy table (RE24) from innings 1–8 gives each outcome's run value; wOBA weights are those values above an out, scaled so league wOBA equals league OBP:

BB 0.731B 0.892B 1.253B 1.64HR 2.01

Projections. Each hitter's outcomes are a chain: strikeout per PA, walk or HBP per non-strikeout, homer per contact, then single, extra-base hit and triple rates on balls in play. Each stage regresses toward the league rate for hitters of the same side. The platoon gap regresses toward the league gap by 2,200 PA against lefties for right-handed and switch hitters, 1,000 for lefties (The Book).

Lineup model. An exact Markov chain over 24 base-out states and nine lineup slots uses 2026 league transitions for each outcome, so double plays, sacrifice flies, errors and extra advancement come from what actually happened. Expected runs per nine innings is solved directly, not simulated. A league-average lineup scores 4.38 per nine against an actual 4.48 per game (98%); the gap is steals, wild pitches and extra innings, which the model leaves out.

Search. Swap and insert moves from The Book's order, the Phillies' order and random starts, kept until no single move helps. The same search re-orders all 162 real lineups against that day's starter hand.

Confidence. 200 bootstrap seasons resample each hitter's plate appearances, re-project and re-score the orders. The share of draws in which the recommended order still beats the Phillies' order is reported with each card.

Limits. No defense, baserunning speed, pinch-hitting, bullpen hand changes or fatigue. Order effects are small by nature, a few runs a season. The offense-first nine ignores defense entirely and is there to price the regulars' gloves.
