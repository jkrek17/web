Once the map stops moving, every forecast step is preloaded as a single image of the view (the bright line under the timeline), so scrubbing and loops are instant. Grids are NDFD forecasts (3-hourly over the oceans, about 72 h ahead). Fronts follow the OPC schedule: analyses every 6 h, and 24/48/72/96 h forecasts. The timeline marks which steps have each.
