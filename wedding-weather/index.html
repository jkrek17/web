How the forecast has changed

Each dot is one model run, oldest on the left. The shaded band is the range: on the rain graphs, from the driest to the wettest of the three ensembles at each run; on the temperature graphs, from 1 in 10 colder to 1 in 10 warmer across all ensemble members. When the band narrows and the newest runs agree with the older ones, the forecast is settling down. The dashed line on the rain graphs is the combined chance, which counts the last three runs of every ensemble with the newest weighted most.
